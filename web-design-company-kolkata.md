## FAQs

### What does a web design company do?

A web design company is a team of designers and developers that work together to deliver unique websites. Pixel Street is the best web design company in Kolkata that integrates brand-centric UI with technical development to produce user-friendly and responsive websites

### What happens during web design consultation?

During our web consultation, we'll discuss about your web design goals, target audience, competition, and timeline for the project. Based on this conversation, we will provide you with a customised proposal and timeline for the project.

### How long will it take to complete my website?

We complete corporate websites usually within 20 working days. As for ecommerce websites, it depends on the number of products, pages and specific functionalities - usually it takes around 30 - 45 days to deliver.

### Do you offer custom designs or use pre-built templates?

We focus on creating custom websites tailored to your brand identity and requirements. We can use pre-built templates if required for faster turnaround times and lower budget projects.

### Will you maintain the website once it is delivered?

Absolutely! We offer Hosting + Maintenance support for all our websites at a fixed monthly fee. Our services include minor design changes, content updates, security, website updates, premium hosting, and SSL.

### Do you offer content writing services for websites?

Yes, we do. Our talented team of content writers create compelling, SEO-optimised content for your website. We work closely with you to understand your target audience, services and brand tone to ensure that the content is compelling.
