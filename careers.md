# Careers at Pixel Street

## Life at Pixel Street

At Pixel Street, our office is more than just a space — it’s where ideas are born, collaborations flourish, and creativity thrives. Ready to be part of our journey? Let’s create something extraordinary together!

## Open Positions

### BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT HEAD

#### Overview

We’re on the hunt for a Business Development Head who has real experience generating leads and closing deals for a creative or advertising agency.

You’ll be the force behind identifying new opportunities, building lasting client relationships, and driving consistent growth. No corporate fluff – just straightforward results.

#### What You’ll Do

- Develop and execute practical, results-driven business strategies that cut through the noise.
- Utilise your network and established channels to source and nurture leads, ensuring a steady pipeline of opportunities.
- Connect authentically with clients and partners, building trust and fostering long-term collaborations.
- Draft proposals and manage the sales process from prospecting to closing.
- Collaborate with the founder and BD team on innovative strategies for expanding our client base.

#### What You Bring

- 5+ years in business development in creative or advertising.
- Proven experience in generating leads and closing deals using established channels.
- Exceptional leadership and communication skills—clear, straightforward, and effective.
- A results-focused mindset with the ability to cut through complexity.

### BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT MANAGER

#### Overview

We are looking for a proactive Business Development Manager who’s all about identifying new opportunities and building the sales pipeline while keeping everything real and results-oriented.

#### What You’ll Do

- Identify and pursue new business prospects that align with our agency’s focus.
- Interact with prospects genuinely, skipping the rehearsed scripts for honest conversation.
- Drive negotiations that secure mutually beneficial arrangements.
- Collaborate closely with our creative and tech groups to develop proposals that hit the mark.
- Maintain an organised sales pipeline and deliver clear, regular updates.

#### What You Bring

- 3–5 years in business development or sales, ideally within a digital agency or startup setting.
- Sharp communication, presentation, and negotiation skills—straightforward and effective.
- A proven record of achieving or surpassing targets with a practical approach.
- Solid project management and time-management skills.

### ART DIRECTOR

#### Overview

We’re looking for an Art Director who can set a clear visual direction for our projects and lead the creative team with practical expertise, not just artistic flair for its own sake.

#### What You’ll Do

- Define the visual direction for the projects in a way that’s creative and results-driven.
- Guide a team of designers, offering candid feedback and actionable direction.
- Work hand-in-hand with copywriters, developers, and strategists to ensure designs add clear value.
- Oversee every step of the creative process, ensuring each project meets our high standards without overcomplicating things.
- Keep projects on track by balancing creative ambitions with deadlines and budgets.

#### What You Bring

- 5+ years in art direction or senior design roles—experience in a digital or creative agency is ideal.
- A portfolio that demonstrates effective design solutions that solve real-world challenges.
- Proficiency in design tools (like Adobe Creative Suite) along with a practical understanding of both digital and print production.
- Well-versed with AI tools like ChatGPT, Midjourney, etc.
- Strong leadership skills that are honest, supportive, and results-oriented.

### SOCIAL MEDIA MANAGER

#### Overview

We are seeking a creative and enthusiastic Social Media Manager to join our awesome marketing team! If you are creative, innovative, and energetic, and if you love attracting people with your writing, we want you on our team!

#### What You’ll Do

- Create and implement social media strategies that are clear, measurable, and true to our brand.
- Work with our creative team to produce engaging, well-crafted content that speaks to real people.
- Build & manage community interactions—responding to comments and building meaningful relationships without the usual hype.
- Use data to understand what’s working and pivot strategies as needed to maximise results.

### SR. WORDPRESS DEVELOPER

We are seeking a Senior WordPress Developer to develop and manage custom WordPress & WooCommerce websites.

You will be responsible for back-end development, including developing WordPress themes and plugins.

#### What You’ll Do

- Partner with design & content teams to create responsive, high-performance WordPress websites that deliver tangible results.
- Craft custom themes and plugins that meet client needs, without overengineering.
- Keep sites fast, secure, and easy to navigate through vigilant performance monitoring and practical optimisations.
