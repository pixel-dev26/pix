# Transforming Logistics Strength into Social Storytelling

Express Roadways, New Delhi

Giving a three-decade-old logistics major a social presence as reliable as its fleet.

- Social Media Management
- Creative Design
- Content Strategy

Manage social media presence and creative output for Express Roadways, one of India's established pan-India logistics and supply chain providers, built on decades of trucking, warehousing and freight-forwarding expertise.

The constraint

Logistics brands tend to disappear online between two extremes: either silence, because the business runs on relationships, not feeds, or generic truck-and-warehouse stock imagery that says nothing about scale, reliability or reach. For a company running thousands of vehicles across a national network, neither extreme reflected the operation.

## The idea

Freight Doesn't Have To Look Like Freight.

Content was built to make the invisible parts of logistics visible: the network, the fleet, the milestones, the people, turning routine operational strength into a social presence people actually noticed.

## Expression

A consistent content and creative system now covers Express Roadways' social channels, translating fleet scale, network reach and operational milestones into visuals and posts built for a B2B and B2C audience alike, maintained on a steady publishing cadence.

“A fleet this size has a story every day. Our job was making sure it got told.”

A more consistent, more visible brand presence for Express Roadways online, reinforcing three decades of operational trust with a digital footprint that finally matches it.
