# Curating Impactful Digital Campaigns for Moroccan Atelier

Maison AhmedFASHION

Turning an atelier's handwork into a social presence that reads as luxury in any language.

- Social Media Strategy
- Content Creation
- Influencer Collaboration

Build Maison Ahmed's international social presence, positioning the Moroccan couture house as a destination for luxury, culturally rooted fashion, and converting engagement into genuine inquiries and sales.

The constraint

Luxury craftsmanship sells through proximity, the closer a customer feels to the making of a piece, the more it's worth to them. Social media is the only channel that can put an international audience that close to an atelier's process, but only if the content earns the same trust the craftsmanship does.

## The idea

Craft, Translated For A Global Feed.

Every piece of content was built to showcase the intricacy of the making, not just the finished garment, treating each campaign, like the Radiant White Harmony series, as a window into the atelier rather than a product post.

## Expression

A tailored social strategy across Instagram and Facebook carried thematic campaigns built around the atelier's craftsmanship, supported by high-quality visuals and narrative content, influencer partnerships that extended reach into new fashion audiences, and ongoing analytics-led optimisation of what was working.

## What we did

- Social Media Strategy DevelopmentWe crafted a tailored social media strategy focusing on platforms like Instagram and Facebook, aligning with Maison Ahmed's brand identity to reach fashion enthusiasts and potential clients.
- Campaign Ideation & ManagementDeveloped and managed thematic campaigns highlighting the atelier's craftsmanship, such as the "Radiant White Harmony" kaftan series, to generate buzz and engagement.
- Content CreationProduced high-quality visuals and compelling narratives that showcased the intricate details of Maison Ahmed's designs, emphasizing their blend of tradition and modernity.
- Influencer CollaborationPartnered with fashion influencers to expand reach and credibility, featuring Maison Ahmed's pieces in diverse styles and settings.
- Analytics and OptimizationMonitored engagement metrics to assess campaign performance, allowing for data-driven adjustments to enhance effectiveness.

“Luxury doesn't need volume. It needs proximity: get people close enough to the craft, and the rest follows.”

A significant rise in followers and engagement across Maison Ahmed's platforms, wider brand recognition among an international audience, and a direct increase in inquiries.

## What it changed

- The strategic social media initiatives led to a significant increase in followers and engagement on Maison Ahmed's platforms. The visually appealing and culturally resonant content attracted a wider audience, enhancing brand recognition and contributing to increased inquiries and sales. This case underscores the importance of a cohesive digital marketing strategy in the luxury fashion sector.
