# Launching an E-Commerce Platform for Innovative Clamps

HelmeClamp Retail

Taking an innovative clamp manufacturer direct to market with its first true e-commerce platform.

- eCommerce
- Web Design
- Product Catalogue

Helme needed a new e-commerce platform built to launch its range of innovative clamps online, giving the brand a direct digital storefront rather than relying solely on offline or third-party channels.

The constraint

A genuinely innovative product still needs a platform that can explain it clearly and sell it directly. The challenge was building an e-commerce experience from the ground up that could carry a technical product range with clarity and ease of navigation for a first-time online buyer.

## The idea

Give The Product Room To Explain Itself.

The platform was built around clear product presentation and straightforward navigation, letting Helme's clamp range speak for itself rather than burying it under unnecessary design flourishes.

## Expression

A dedicated e-commerce website was designed and developed for Helme, structured to showcase its product catalogue clearly and support a smooth path from browsing to purchase.

## What we did

- UI DesignWe developed a sleek, modern user interface reflecting Helme's innovative brand. The clean and intuitive design made it easy for customers to navigate and find the products they needed.
- WooCommerce DevelopmentThe website was built on WooCommerce, customized to handle Helme’s unique requirements such as custom STP files for each product, detailed installation guides, and advanced filtering options to help users find specific clamps quickly.
- Regional SettingsWe implemented a system that adjusts pricing based on the user's region, including specific shipping rates and GST calculations. This ensured that customers saw accurate pricing and tax information based on their location.
- Payment Gateway IntegrationThe payment gateway was integrated with Helme’s bank, enabling secure transactions. We also set up automated, customized emails for order confirmations, shipping updates, and customer support.
- Launch and OptimizationAfter overcoming the delays, we successfully launched the website. Post-launch, we continued to optimize the site for performance and SEO, ensuring smooth navigation and transaction processes.

“A genuinely new product doesn't need a loud website. It needs one that gets out of the way and lets the product make its case.”

A new, fully functional e-commerce platform that gave Helme's innovative clamp range a direct digital storefront for the first time.

## What it changed

- Despite manufacturing and photography delays, our team delivered a robust platform that met Helme’s needs. The customizations and attention to detail ensured that the website was not only functional but also provided a superior user experience.
- This project demonstrated our ability to handle complex e-commerce requirements and deliver high-quality solutions, even under challenging circumstances.
