# Engineering a Digital Experience for Lifting & Rigging Solutions

EMM Corp — Lifting & Rigging

- E-Commerce
- UI/UX Design
- Development

EMM Corp specialises in lifting, rigging, wire rope, marine mooring and offshore solutions across marine, industrial, energy and offshore environments. With a wide, technical product and service portfolio, EMM needed its website completely revamped into a modern, structured, experience-led platform reflecting its engineering expertise and safety-first approach.

Context & Insight

EMM operates in an industry where customers need to understand technical products, certifications and capabilities before they’ll even consider a decision. The challenge was never how much information to include, it was organising genuine technical depth, wire ropes, lifting and rigging, marine and mooring, inspection, certification, asset management, without overwhelming a visitor who arrived looking for one specific answer.

## What we did

- A Layered User Journey: Rebuilt the site around Discover, Understand, Explore, Evaluate, Connect, moving visitors from a high-level overview into deep technical detail only when they actually needed it.
- Web Design & Development: Designed and developed a modern, structured B2B platform, information architecture, visual system and front-end build, engineered to hold serious technical depth without overwhelming a first-time visitor.
- Services Structured Around The Equipment Lifecycle: Organised inspection, certification, asset management and rental equipment to position EMM as a long-term technical partner, not just a supplier. Let the same technical capabilities speak differently to ports, drilling, offshore energy and renewables, each entering through what’s actually relevant to their operation.

## Impact

A clearer, more contemporary B2B digital presence for EMM Corp, with dedicated journeys across products, services and markets, real engineering work showcased as proof of capability, and a scalable system built to grow alongside the business.
