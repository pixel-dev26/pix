# Design-Driven Launch for an Innovative Agarbatti Variant

Mangaldeep AgarbattiFMCG

Building a dedicated creative pod that could move at FMCG speed without ever drifting off Mangaldeep's brand guidelines.

- Packaging Design
- Catalogue Design
- POSM
- Promotional Films
- Photoshoots

Mangaldeep Agarbatti has trusted us as a long-term creative partner for over three years running across the 3in1, Scent 3in1, Flora, Sandal and Temple ranges, spanning packaging, catalogues, POSM, promotional videos, print collateral and photoshoots, all delivered at the pace a competitive, fast-moving FMCG category demands, without letting speed cost the work its quality.

The constraint

As one of India's most recognised agarbatti brands, Mangaldeep continuously introduces new variants, festive editions, and retail campaigns throughout the year. Each initiative demanded fresh creative execution without losing the familiarity and trust consumers associate with the brand. The challenge wasn't simply producing creative assets, it was building a streamlined creative ecosystem capable of delivering at speed while maintaining premium quality and complete brand consistency.

## The idea

One Creative Team.

Endless Possibilities.

Instead of approaching every project as a standalone assignment, we became an extension of Mangaldeep's marketing team. A dedicated creative unit comprising strategists, designers, copywriters and motion specialists worked together to ensure every package, catalogue, retail display, promotional film and campaign asset reflected the same visual language and brand philosophy. This collaborative approach enabled faster execution, smoother approvals and creative consistency across every touchpoint.

## Expression

Every asset, packaging redesigns, seasonal catalogues, in-store POSM, festive promotional films, full product photoshoots, was built against Mangaldeep's brand guidelines with the same discipline, regardless of which of the five ranges it belonged to or how quickly it needed to ship. That combination, a dedicated team, a tight communication loop and unwavering guideline adherence, is what lets three years of fast-moving output stay recognisably, unmistakably Mangaldeep.

## What we did

- Team FormationWe assembled a dedicated team comprising a creative head, a copywriter, two graphic designers, and a video animator, ensuring that specialised skills were directed towards each aspect of the creative process.
- Communication & CollaborationMangaldeep’s brand team established a priority communication group, enabling real-time feedback and approvals, which greatly streamlined the creative process.
- Brand Guidelines AdherenceWe meticulously followed Mangaldeep product brand guidelines, ensuring every piece of content resonated with the brand's core identity and messaging strategy.

“A brand people lit in their homes every single day earns a different kind of loyalty. Our job for over three years has been to protect that trust in every single asset.”

Three years and counting as Mangaldeep's creative partner across five product ranges and every major retail and print touchpoint.

## What it changed

- Timely DeliveryOur ability to deliver designs on a tight schedule ensured that Mangaldeep's creative requirements were never delayed and were in sync with their strategic timelines.
- Consistent VisualsThe adherence to brand guidelines and the expertise of our dedicated team ensured a seamless and consistent visual identity across all creative assets.
- Market ImpactOur compelling and timely creative materials helped in marketing and creating impact for the products and messaging.
- Our ongoing partnership as the go-to agency for Mangaldeep Agarbatti’s creative needs continues to flourish, driving brand loyalty and customer satisfaction through each thoughtfully designed piece of collateral.
