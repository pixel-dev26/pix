# Crafting Digital-First Visuals for an FMCG Giant

Marico LimitedFMCG

Bringing distinct brand equity into a cluttered online marketplace, product page by product page. AMAZON A+ CONTENT, PDP ARTWORK, KEY VISUALS, POSM, BANNERS

- Key Visuals
- Marketing Collaterals
- Banners

Marico Limited, one of India's leading FMCG giants, entrusted us with designing and developing artwork for its brands, Herbs India, Parachute and COCOIndia, spanning Amazon A+ content, PDP artwork, compelling key visuals, POSM artworks and banners that bring each brand's core proposition to life across online marketplaces and brand campaigns.

The constraint

A cluttered online marketplace punishes generic design. The real challenge was never producing visuals at scale, it was maintaining each brand's individual essence and equity while still aligning with e-commerce best practices and consumer psychology, so Parachute, Herbs India and COCOIndia could each keep their own voice inside one shared discipline of what actually converts on a product page.

## The idea

Decode The Brand Before You Design The Page.

Every engagement began with in-depth brand immersion sessions to decode each brand's positioning and consumer voice, so the visual system that followed wasn't a generic e-commerce template stretched across three brands, it was built from what each one already meant to its own consumer.

## Expression

A+ content artwork broke product benefits down into digestible visuals: ingredient highlights, consumer concerns and brand USPs, while PDP imagery blended lifestyle context with product close-ups to simulate real-world usage. Alongside the marketplace work, POSM artworks and banners extended the same brand discipline into Marico's COCOIndia campaigns.

## What we did

- Conducted in-depth brand immersion sessions to decode each brand's positioning and consumer voice.
- Developed A+ content artwork that broke down product benefits into digestible visuals—using ingredient highlights, consumer concerns, and brand USPs.
- Designed PDP imagery that seamlessly blends lifestyle context with product close-ups to simulate real-world usage.
- Built Key Visuals with adaptability in mind—usable across marketplaces, social media, and in-store displays for extended brand recall.

“On a cluttered product page, you don't get to explain the brand. The image has to already know what it stands for.”

A platform-first, digital-forward visual system across Marico's brand portfolio, proof that even inside a marketplace's rigid template, a brand's essence doesn't have to disappear.

## What it changed

- Stronger Visual Differentiation The distinct storytelling for each brand helped them stand out in an otherwise saturated category.
- Consistent Brand LanguageKey Visuals unified the brand narrative across platforms, strengthening overall recall.
- Positive Internal ReceptionBoth internal brand and category teams at Marico appreciated the clarity, coherence, and visual impact across deliverables.
- By leveraging platform-first design and emotionally resonant storytelling, we created a seamless digital experience for Marico’s consumers. This project demonstrates how thoughtful visual identity—rooted in brand essence—can drive both relevance and results in e-commerce ecosystems.
