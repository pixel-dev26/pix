# Creating Consistent Social & Email Communication For Satguru Russia

Satguru Travels, Russia

Managing Satguru Travels' presence across the platforms Russian travellers actually use, VK, Telegram and Max, alongside the emailers that keep them booking.

- Social Media Management
- Emailer Design

Manage Satguru Travels' social media presence across Russia's actual platform ecosystem, VK, Telegram and Max, alongside ongoing emailer design, giving the brand a consistent voice across every channel its Russian audience is actually on.

The constraint

A Russian audience doesn't live on Instagram or Facebook the way most international travel brands assume. VK, Telegram and Max are where the real conversations, communities and travel planning actually happen. For a global travel brand entering that market, the challenge wasn't translating existing content from elsewhere, it was building a presence native to platforms with their own formats, pacing and audience behaviour.

## The idea

Build For The Platform, Not Around It.

Rather than repurpose one global content calendar across every channel, content was shaped around what actually works on each platform, VK's community and group culture, Telegram's direct, channel-based updates, Max's newer, fast-growing audience, so the brand felt native to its Russian audience rather than visiting from somewhere else.

## Expression

Ongoing social media management across VK, Telegram and Max keeps Satguru Travels visible on the channels its Russian audience actually spends time on, each platform handled on its own terms rather than as a copy-paste of the others. Emailer design carries that same visual language into the inbox, so itineraries, offers and travel inspiration read consistently whether a traveller finds Satguru on a channel or in their inbox.

“A travel brand entering Russia doesn't need a translated feed, it needs a native one. VK, Telegram and Max aren't alternatives to Instagram, they are the actual channel.”

A consistent, platform-native social and email presence for Satguru Travels across Russia's real digital ecosystem, keeping the brand visible and bookable wherever its audience actually is.
