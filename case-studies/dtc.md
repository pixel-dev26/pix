# Unifying a Diverse Portfolio with a Revamped Website

DTC GroupReal Estate

Unifying mining, real estate and infrastructure under a single, credible digital identity.

- Corporate Website
- Brand System
- Lead Generation
- DTC Capital City
- Sojon
- Southern Heights

Redesign the DTC Group website to represent a genuinely diversified conglomerate, mining, real estate, infrastructure, under one unified brand, while launching major real estate ventures: DTC Capital City, Sojon and Southern Heights.

The constraint

Diversified groups carry a specific credibility problem online, too many business lines told through too many disconnected pages, leaving visitors unsure whether they're looking at one serious company or three unrelated ones. DTC's scale needed to read as strength, not scatter.

## The idea

Same Company, Every Page.

One disciplined brand system, palette, type, imagery, was applied across every division and project page, so the Group's legacy and credibility carried through no matter which business line a visitor landed on.

## Expression

A custom, minimalist WordPress build carried DTC's brand guidelines across corporate pages on history, vision, mission and values, alongside dedicated media and CSR sections. Detailed project pages for DTC Capital City, Sojon and Southern Heights covered amenities, pricing, floor plans and enquiry forms, with custom lead forms wired into a unified CRM via Zapier and backed by technical and on-page SEO.

## What we did

- Brand Guidelines ImplementationWe ensured consistent use of DTC's brand guidelines across the website by incorporating the approved colour palette, fonts, and imagery, thus providing a cohesive visual identity.
- Custom WordPress DevelopmentA customized WordPress CMS was developed to accommodate DTC Group's website structure and content needs. This allowed for easy content updates and seamless integration of new projects as they were launched.
- Project Listing & DetailsThe website included detailed project pages for Capital City, Sojon, and Southern Heights, each providing comprehensive information on amenities, pricing, floor plans, and enquiry forms to facilitate lead generation.
- Corporate Information PagesWe designed pages to effectively present the group's history, vision, mission, and values, reinforcing DTC Group’s legacy and credibility in the industry.
- InMedia & CSRDedicated sections were created for media and CSR information, highlighting awards, recognition, and corporate social responsibility initiatives.
- Custom Forms with Zapier & SEOWe integrated custom forms integration with Zapier for a unified lead CRM and ensured technical and on-page SEO optimisation to improve search engine visibility.
- DeploymentThe website was deployed on a stable and secure hosting environment, ensuring reliable performance.

“Scale should read as strength. If a visitor can't tell it's one company, the website hasn't done its job.”

A stronger, more cohesive brand identity across the Group's portfolio, sharper engagement on its flagship real estate projects, and one unified lead pipeline in place of several fragmented ones.

## What it changed

- Enhanced Brand IdentityThe unified visual identity across the site strengthened the group's overall brand and helped position it as a leader.
- Increased EngagementThe comprehensive project pages facilitated higher engagement and enquiry rates for the real estate projects.
- Seamless User ExperienceThe responsive design and intuitive navigation allowed for a seamless user experience across devices.
- Stronger Corporate MessagingThe website effectively communicated DTC Group's values and legacy, building greater trust with stakeholders.
- The DTC Group website project highlighted the importance of aligning digital strategies with corporate goals.
- By integrating multiple business divisions and showcasing diverse projects, we were able to create a cohesive platform that not only informs but also engages potential clients and investors. Our approach underscored the value of custom development and strategic content planning to build a strong, unified brand presence across varied markets.
