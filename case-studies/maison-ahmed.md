# Let The Craft Speak

Maison AhmedFashion & Lifestyle

An e-commerce platform built to sell handcrafted Moroccan kaftans to a genuinely global customer.

- eCommerce
- Woocommerce
- Multilingual Ux
- AI Chatbot

Build an international e-commerce platform for a Moroccan couture atelier specialising in handcrafted kaftans and bridal wear, supporting a global, multilingual, multi-currency clientele without compromising the brand's minimalist elegance.

The constraint

Craftsmanship-led fashion brands face a particular tension online, the product is tactile, cultural and detailed, everything a screen struggles to convey, while the audience is scattered across markets that share neither a language nor a currency.

## The idea

Let The Craft Speak, Let The Platform Get Out Of The Way.

The interface was stripped back to near-nothing so nothing competed with the product photography, then the complexity, languages, currencies, customer support, was built in invisibly, underneath.

## Expression

A minimalist WooCommerce build supported multiple languages, including English, French and Arabic, and multiple currencies, including MAD, EUR, USD, SAR and AED, for an international clientele, paired with an AI-powered multilingual chatbot giving non-English-speaking customers real-time support, and a streamlined backend simplifying inventory, orders and communication.

## What we did

- Minimalistic UI/UX DesignWe crafted a clean, minimalistic design for the WooCommerce website, emphasizing visual simplicity to highlight Maison Ahmed's luxurious products. The design uses high-quality images and elegant typography to convey the brand’s sophisticated aesthetic.
- Multilingual & Multi-Currency SupportUnderstanding the diverse customer base, the website was developed to support multiple languages, including English, French, Arabic, and the currencies MAD, EUR, USD, SAR, and AED. This ensures that shoppers from different regions can browse and shop with ease in their native language and currency.
- Custom WooCommerce IntegrationThe WooCommerce platform was customized to streamline the management of products, orders, and customer interactions, tailored specifically to the needs of a high-fashion retailer.
- Advanced Chatbot with AI TranslationWe integrated a sophisticated chatbot that uses Gen AI technology for real-time translation. This tool can converse with customers in multiple languages, answering queries about products.
- Mobile OptimizationThe website was developed in mobile-first approach with fast loading times and responsiveness across all platforms.

“When the product is this considered, the website's only job is to stay out of its way.”

Broader international reach, longer on-site engagement, and smoother day-to-day operations, all running through one platform built for a global customer.

## What it changed

- Enhanced Global ReachThe multilingual and multi-currency functionalities have effectively broadened Maison Ahmed’s market, attracting customers from various international locations.
- Increased Customer EngagementThe minimalist design and high usability of the website have led to increased customer engagement, with visitors spending more time exploring the collections and making purchases.
- Improved Customer SupportThe AI-powered chatbot has significantly improved customer support, providing instant assistance and enriching the shopping experience for non-English speakers.
- Streamlined OperationsThe custom WooCommerce setup allows for efficient management of the online store, simplifying processes like inventory management, order processing, and customer communications.
- This project highlighted the importance of combining aesthetic design with functional sophistication in creating an e-commerce platform for a luxury fashion brand.
- Maison Ahmed is well-positioned to cater to its upscale, global clientele while maintaining the brand’s heritage and luxury appeal.
