# Building a Digital Storefront Worthy of a Heritage Jeweller

Nemichand Bamalwa & Sons — Jewellery Retail

- Web Design
- UX
- Local SEO

Nemichand Bamalwa & Sons, a heritage fine jewellery house on Park Street, Kolkata since 1987, needed a website built to match the weight of that legacy, one that could present bridal, wedding, soirée and everyday collections with equal elegance, while giving a genuinely old house a digital presence that felt current, not preserved.

Context & Insight

Heritage jewellers face a specific design trap online: lean too hard into tradition and the brand looks dated; lean too hard into modern minimalism and decades of craft and trust disappear into a generic template. For a house that’s been featured in Vogue, Harper’s Bazaar and GQ, the website needed to read as premium as the coverage, without losing the warmth of a family business three generations deep.

## What we did

- Website Design & Development: Built the entire site from the ground up, structure, UI/UX and front-end development, designed to carry three decades of heritage with a genuinely premium, modern feel.
- Legacy & Brand Storytelling: Built a dedicated Legacy section around Heritage, Craft, Integrity and Timelessness, giving the house’s history room to actually be told, not just claimed.
- Appointment & Enquiry Flow: Built a direct booking and enquiry path, keeping the digital experience grounded in how a jewellery purchase actually happens, in person.

## Impact

A digital presence built to match Nemichand Bamalwa & Sons’ standing as one of Kolkata’s most trusted names in fine jewellery, giving three decades of heritage a home online that feels as premium, curated and story-led as the brand itself.
