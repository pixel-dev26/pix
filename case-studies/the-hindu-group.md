# Boosting Onboarding & Retention via Creative Email Journey

The Hindu GroupMedia & Publishing

Helping India's most trusted publishing house turn readers into subscribers, and subscribers into renewals.

- Email Campaigns
- Digital Advertisements
- Marketing Collateral

The Hindu Group, publisher of The Hindu, Frontline and BusinessLine since 1878, brought us on to strengthen digital engagement across its e-paper subscription business: email campaigns and marketing collateral built around the full customer journey, from first sign-up through onboarding, retention and renewal, in a rapidly changing media landscape.

The constraint

Few brands carry the weight of history The Hindu Group does, nearly a century and a half of continuous publishing, and few face a sharper modern pivot: turning that legacy trust into digital subscriptions in a market where attention is won by newsletters and app notifications, not the morning doorstep. A dedicated team of strategists, designers and HTML developers was formed to tailor and execute campaigns for each stage of that customer journey.

## The idea

Trusted, Not Tired.

Rather than designing isolated campaigns, we developed a flexible creative approach that could scale across multiple brands and formats. Every email campaign, digital advertisement and piece of marketing collateral was crafted to balance editorial credibility with modern digital communication, ensuring every interaction reinforced trust while driving reader engagement and subscription growth.

## Expression

Our partnership covered a wide range of creative deliverables, including email campaigns, digital advertisements, promotional creatives and marketing collateral for The Hindu, Frontline and BusinessLine. Each publication retained its unique personality while following a unified creative system, enabling faster campaign execution, stronger visual consistency and seamless communication across the Group's digital ecosystem.

## What we did

- Campaign DevelopmentWe designed targeted campaigns addressing specific needs at each stage of their journey with THG.
- Team FormationA dedicated team of strategists, designers, and HTML developers was formed to tailor and execute these strategies effectively.
- EmailersWe revamped the email communication strategy, creating compelling onboarding, retention, and renewal journeys. This involved sophisticated design and HTML coding for impactful delivery.
- Communication & CollaborationWe maintained a dynamic communication channel with THG’s marketing team via Google Chat, enabling quick feedback and iterative enhancements to the campaigns.
- Brand Guidelines AdherenceConsistent with THG’s esteemed reputation, we adhered strictly to their brand guidelines across all materials, ensuring that each campaign reflected the brand’s journalistic excellence.

“A brand with a 148-year-old legacy doesn't need reinvention, it needs translation, into whatever format its readers are on today.”

A consistent, subscription-focused creative system supporting The Hindu Group's digital growth across its mastheads.

## What it changed

- Enhanced EngagementOur redesigned emailers and digital campaigns improved engagement metrics across the customer lifecycle.
- Increased Renewal RatesThe tailored renewal campaigns we developed led to a marked increase in subscription renewals, contributing to sustained readership growth.
- Consistent Brand ExperienceWe ensured a consistent and professional brand experience through unified visual designs.
- Our partnership with The Hindu Group highlights the importance of evolving digital strategies to expand its leadership in the e-paper subscription space. We will continue driving innovation and growth for The Hindu Group's campaigns.
