Unifying a Diverse Portfolio with a Revamped Website

Unifying mining, real estate and infrastructure under a single, credible digital identity.

  • Corporate Website
  • Brand System
  • Lead Generation
  • DTC Capital City
  • Sojon
  • Southern Heights

The brief

Redesign the DTC Group website to represent a genuinely diversified conglomerate, mining, real estate, infrastructure, under one unified brand, while launching major real estate ventures: DTC Capital City, Sojon and Southern Heights.

The constraint

Diversified groups carry a specific credibility problem online, too many business lines told through too many disconnected pages, leaving visitors unsure whether they're looking at one serious company or three unrelated ones. DTC's scale needed to read as strength, not scatter.

What we did

  • Brand Guidelines Implementation

    We ensured consistent use of DTC's brand guidelines across the website by incorporating the approved colour palette, fonts, and imagery, thus providing a cohesive visual identity.

  • Custom WordPress Development

    A customized WordPress CMS was developed to accommodate DTC Group's website structure and content needs. This allowed for easy content updates and seamless integration of new projects as they were launched.

  • Project Listing & Details

    The website included detailed project pages for Capital City, Sojon, and Southern Heights, each providing comprehensive information on amenities, pricing, floor plans, and enquiry forms to facilitate lead generation.

  • Corporate Information Pages

    We designed pages to effectively present the group's history, vision, mission, and values, reinforcing DTC Group’s legacy and credibility in the industry.

  • InMedia & CSR

    Dedicated sections were created for media and CSR information, highlighting awards, recognition, and corporate social responsibility initiatives.

  • Custom Forms with Zapier & SEO

    We integrated custom forms integration with Zapier for a unified lead CRM and ensured technical and on-page SEO optimisation to improve search engine visibility.

  • Deployment

    The website was deployed on a stable and secure hosting environment, ensuring reliable performance.

What it changed

  • Enhanced Brand Identity

    The unified visual identity across the site strengthened the group's overall brand and helped position it as a leader.

  • Increased Engagement

    The comprehensive project pages facilitated higher engagement and enquiry rates for the real estate projects.

  • Seamless User Experience

    The responsive design and intuitive navigation allowed for a seamless user experience across devices.

  • Stronger Corporate Messaging

    The website effectively communicated DTC Group's values and legacy, building greater trust with stakeholders.

  • The DTC Group website project highlighted the importance of aligning digital strategies with corporate goals.

  • By integrating multiple business divisions and showcasing diverse projects, we were able to create a cohesive platform that not only informs but also engages potential clients and investors. Our approach underscored the value of custom development and strategic content planning to build a strong, unified brand presence across varied markets.
