# Reimagining Web Design for Integrated Logistics Marketplace

ShipzipLogistics

Making a tech-driven logistics network as legible online as it is on the road.

- Web Design
- Custom Illustration
- Content Strategy

Rebuild ShipZip's website to position it as a modern, tech-enabled logistics and supply chain marketplace, spanning First Mile, Mid Mile, Last Mile, 3PL warehousing and hyper-local delivery.

The constraint

Logistics is genuinely difficult to visualise, it's trucks, warehouses, routing and SLAs, none of which photograph well. Most logistics websites default to generic stock imagery of vehicles and never quite explain what actually happens between pickup and delivery.

## The idea

Draw What You Can't Photograph.

Stock imagery was replaced with custom illustration built specifically to explain ShipZip's service layers, turning an operationally complex business into something a first-time visitor could understand in a scroll.

## Expression

An intuitive UI/UX served both B2B and B2C visitors, a custom illustration system broke down ShipZip's service architecture into relatable visuals, a dedicated blog positioned the brand as a logistics thought leader, and form submissions were wired directly into a live sales tracking sheet.

## What we did

- UI/UX DesignWe developed a user interface that is both intuitive and engaging. The design focuses on easy navigation making it simple for both B2B and B2C clients to find the information they need.
- Custom IllustrationsTo vividly convey the dynamic nature of ShipZip's operations and services, we created custom illustrations that depict various aspects of the logistics process. These visuals help to break down complex logistics operations into understandable and relatable content.
- Blogs DevelopmentWe developed a dedicated blog section to educate visitors about logistics trends, tips, and the latest industry news, positioning ShipZip as a thought leader in the logistics space.
- Form to Google Sheet IntegrationWe integrated a system where form submissions are directly linked to a Google Sheet. This setup enhances the efficiency of customer service and sales follow-ups.

“Nobody can photograph a supply chain. So we drew one they could actually understand.”

An elevated brand image reflecting ShipZip's status as a tech-driven logistics leader, stronger engagement across trade and retail audiences, and a faster enquiry-to-follow-up pipeline for the sales team.

## What it changed

- Enhanced Brand IdentityThe refined brand guidelines and custom illustrations elevated ShipZip’s online image, reflecting its status as a tech-driven logistics leader.
- Improved User EngagementThe new UI/UX design led to an increase in website engagement, with visitors spending more time exploring the services and solutions offered.
- Effective Lead ManagementThe integration of forms with Google Sheets optimized the lead management process, enabling quicker response times and better customer interaction.
- Boost in SEO and Content ReachThe strategically developed blog section not only enhanced the website's SEO but also established ShipZip as an authority in the logistics sector, attracting more organic traffic.
- By combining user-centric design principles with technical functionality, we delivered a robust digital platform that not only meets the immediate needs of ShipZip but also supports its long-term vision of leading the logistics sector through technological advancement.
