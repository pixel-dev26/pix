# Winning the Aisle with Attractive Point-of-Sale Design

Turning point-of-sale into ITC Foods' loudest, clearest voice in-store.

- POSM Design
- Key Visuals
- Poster Design
- Retail Creative

Design point-of-sale materials, in-store posters and key visuals for ITC Foods, assets that had to work not on a screen with a scroll button, but on a crowded retail shelf competing for a two-second glance while a shopper walks past.

The constraint

Retail POSM is an entirely different discipline from digital creative, there's no swipe to bring the shopper back, no second view. A poster either stops someone within three feet of the aisle or it doesn't. For a house of brands as large as ITC Foods, the real task was building a visual system flexible enough to flex across categories and campaigns, yet disciplined enough to always read as unmistakably ITC.

## The idea

Stop, Then Sell.

Every key visual was built backwards from stopping power first, one dominant, appetite-triggering hero shot, and the sell second, with a clean hierarchy guiding the eye from craving to conversion in the time it takes to walk past a shelf.

## Expression

From large-format posters to shelf-level POSM, typography stayed bold and legible from a distance, product photography rich enough to trigger appetite appeal, and layouts built to survive the visual noise of a real Indian retail environment.

“POSM doesn't get a scroll or a swipe, it gets a glance. We designed every visual to win in that one glance.”

A POSM and key-visual system now deployed across ITC Foods' retail footprint.
