# Building a High-Impact Creative System

Keeping a century-old premium mixer brand looking premium, even on a ten-minute-delivery banner.

Schweppes needed its premium positioning to be protected across every fast-moving digital touchpoint, including quick-commerce banners, social media posts, POSM, and AI-generated videos, without the platforms’ own pace and clutter diluting the brand’s upscale feel.

Context & insight

Quick commerce and social media are built for speed and volume; premium brands are built on the opposite instinct, restraint, patience, and craft. Most brands entering these platforms end up looking like everything else on them. Schweppes’ challenge was staying visibly premium inside environments engineered to move fast and look loud.

## What we did

- Quick Commerce Creative. Built banners and assets holding a restrained, premium visual discipline even inside a fast, cluttered app environment.
- AI Video Production. Scaled premium motion content, muted golds, crisp glass reflections, at a volume traditional production couldn’t sustain.
- Social Content. Carried the same tonal control across social-first assets, keeping the brand consistent from app to feed.
- POSM. Extended Schweppes’ premium visual language into in-store presence, not just digital touchpoints.

## Impact

A premium creative system now runs consistently across Schweppes’ quick-commerce, social and in-store presence, holding the brand’s upscale identity together across formats engineered for speed rather than restraint.
