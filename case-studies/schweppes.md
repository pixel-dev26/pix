# Building a High-Impact Creative System

Keeping a century-old premium mixer brand looking premium, even on a ten-minute-delivery banner.

- Quick Commerce
- AI Video
- Social Content
- POSM

Schweppes needed its premium positioning to be protected across every fast-moving digital touchpoint, including quick-commerce banners, social media posts, POSM and AI-generated videos, without the platforms' own pace and clutter diluting the brand's upscale feel.

The constraint

Quick commerce and social media are built for speed and volume, premium brands are built on the opposite instinct: restraint, patience and craft. Most brands entering these platforms end up looking like everything else on them. Schweppes' challenge was staying visibly premium inside environments engineered to move fast and look loud.

## The idea

Premium Brand, Elevated Visuals.

We built a visual discipline, restrained colour, considered negative space, a lighting language borrowed from premium spirits photography, and applied it without compromise across every fast platform Schweppes needed to appear on, proving premium doesn't have to mean slow-moving.

## Expression

From quick-commerce banners to social-first assets, every touchpoint carried the same tonal control: muted golds, crisp glass reflections and minimal typography. AI-generated visuals and video were integrated to scale premium motion content at a volume traditional production couldn't sustain, without compromising the brand's upscale finish.

“Premium doesn't get diluted by the platform, it gets diluted by the creative. We simply refused to let that happen.”

A premium creative system now running consistently across Schweppes' quick-commerce, social and in-store presence.
