# Building a Digital Backbone for Connection at Scale

CrizacEducation

One consistent identity, carried from a public-facing website into a private CRM portal.

- Brand Identity Design
- Website Development
- CRM Portal Development
- Unified Branding

Crizac needed a visual identity and a set of digital platforms, a public website and an internal CRM portal, built to embody its core values of connectivity, simplicity and support.

The constraint

Platforms with both a public and a private side face a specific consistency problem: the polished, persuasive face a new user meets on the website has to hold together with the efficient, detail-heavy interface staff use every day inside the CRM. Most brands let those two experiences drift apart.

## The idea

One Brand, Two Interfaces, No Compromise.

Rather than treat the CRM as an internal tool exempt from brand thinking, the identity, a bold, modern typeface paired with an arrow motif symbolising progress and ambition, was applied with the same discipline across both the public website and the CRM portal.

## Expression

The public website was built as a dynamic, user-friendly interface for new users, with clear navigation and interactive content highlighting Crizac's key features and advantages. The CRM portal, focused squarely on usability, used a clean layout and a thoughtful colour palette to support efficient task and workflow management while minimising user fatigue.

## What we did

- Brand Identity DesignWe developed a visual identity for Crizac that embodies its core values of connectivity, simplicity, and support. The logo features a bold, modern typeface and an arrow motif, symbolizing progress and ambition.
- Website DevelopmentWe crafted a dynamic, user-friendly website that serves as the primary interface for new users. The site is designed with clear navigation and interactive content that highlights Crizac’s key features and advantages.
- CRM Portal DevelopmentFocused on usability, the CRM portal’s interface supports efficient management of detailed tasks and workflows. The design utilizes a clean layout and a thoughtful color palette to ensure readability and minimize user fatigue.
- Unified BrandingWe ensured that Crizac’s branding was consistently applied across all digital platforms, including the CRM portal, to facilitate a seamless user experience from public-facing content to private interactions.

“The version of your brand a new user sees and the version your own team works in all day should never feel like two different companies.”

A seamless user experience from public-facing content to private, day-to-day interactions, one consistently applied identity across every digital touchpoint Crizac operates.

## What it changed

- Crizac’s IPO was a milestone achievement, and the branding and UI work received acclaim for its role in enhancing user experience and company valuation. The company has noted an increase in engagement from universities and partners, citing the ease of use and compelling design as key factors in their continued success. The project exemplifies the power of thoughtful branding and UX design in supporting business growth and user satisfaction in the tech-driven world of international education.
