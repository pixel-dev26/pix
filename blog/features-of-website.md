# 18 Features of a Website That Drive Massive Results

Eighteen features, one list, every item weighted the same. That is the lie built into this format, and I have written enough lists like it to own the criticism.

So here is what I actually think, from a studio in Kolkata that builds these for a living. Three items below decide whether a website works: what it says that nobody else could say, whether it stays fast and stable on a mid-range phone, and whether it tells a visitor what to do next. The other fifteen are hygiene. Hygiene is not optional, and a site with no SSL certificate or a broken contact page is a broken site. But nobody has ever chosen a supplier because the footer had a sitemap in it.

![Three features decide whether a website works: saying something no competitor could say, staying fast and stable on a mid-range phone, and telling a visitor what to do next. The other fifteen, from brand logo to site search, are hygiene: not optional, but not why anyone chose a supplier.](https://pixelstreet.in/blog/diagrams/features-of-website-three-and-fifteen.svg)

Read the list as a checklist for the fifteen and an argument for the three. Every number below was checked against a primary source on 31 July 2026, and where the standard claim has no source behind it, I say so instead of repeating it.

## Brand Name and Logo

### Why are your brand name and logo essential?

Your brand name and logo are the first things visitors should notice when they arrive on your website. They are key to establishing brand recognition and trust. Your brand name should be prominently displayed on your home or landing page—ideally at the top, in the middle, or left corner—so it’s immediately visible without scrolling.

### Tips for Enhancing Brand Presence:

- **Strategic Placement:** Put the brand name at the top of every page, in the header, so it stays visible as people move around.
- **Consistent Branding:** Use a logo that complements the name rather than competing with it.
- **Professional Design:** If the logo is still a placeholder, get it designed properly before you build around it.

## Personalized Domain Name

### Why is a personalized domain name important?

Your domain is how people find you and how they repeat you to somebody else. A free subdomain with the host’s name in it announces that the business has not committed to itself yet.

### Tips for Choosing the Right Domain Name:

- **Keep It Simple:** Easy to spell and easy to say aloud, because people will have to do both.
- **Be Unique:** Simple, but still distinguishable from your competitors.
- **Consider Purchasing:** If the one you want is taken, buying it from the owner is an option, and so is a closely related alternative.

## Clear and Concise Navigation

### Why is website navigation important?

Navigation is the map. If a visitor cannot see where the thing they came for lives, they leave, and no amount of design elsewhere compensates.

### Tips for Effective Navigation:

- **Label things plainly:** Name the destination, not the department that owns it.
- **Keep the menu short:** Every extra item makes the others harder to see.
- **Add a search bar** if the site is content-heavy.
- **Keep it identical on every page.**

## Responsive Design for All Devices

### What is a responsive website design?

A responsive design automatically adjusts your website’s layout based on the device being used, whether it’s a desktop, tablet, or smartphone. With more people accessing websites on mobile devices, responsive design is crucial for providing a seamless user experience.

### Benefits of Responsive Design:

- **Enhanced user experience:** Users will have a consistent experience, regardless of the device they’re using.
- **Indexing, not a bonus:** Google crawls with its smartphone agent, and its documentation is blunt about the consequence: “Only the content shown on the mobile site is used for indexing.” Anything you hide on small screens to tidy the layout is content Google never reads. That is the mechanism. There is no separate reward for being mobile-friendly.
- **Fewer people leaving:** A layout that works on the device somebody is actually holding is a layout they stay on.

One thing this list left out entirely, and should not have. Responsive is not the same as accessible, and accessibility stopped being a nice-to-have. WCAG 2.2 has been a W3C Recommendation since 5 October 2023, republished on 12 December 2024 with errata and not a single success criterion added or renumbered — so if a proposal quotes you only the 2024 date, it is describing a standard that is nearly three years old, not a fresh one. The European Accessibility Act has applied since 28 June 2025, which reaches Indian businesses selling into the EU whether or not they have noticed. The mechanics are in [our guide to website accessibility compliance](https://pixelstreet.in/blog/website-accessibility-compliance/), and the layout side is in [the responsive web design guide](https://pixelstreet.in/blog/responsive-web-design/).

## Fast Loading Speed

### How does loading speed affect website performance?

Speed drives bounces and feeds ranking, and it has published thresholds now instead of folklore. You will read everywhere that research shows users expect a site to load in under 3 seconds. I went looking for that research and could not find it, so I do not quote it.

What does exist is Google’s Core Web Vitals, and the numbers are specific. Largest Contentful Paint should occur within **2.5 seconds**. Interaction to Next Paint should be **200 milliseconds** or less. Cumulative Layout Shift should be **0.1** or less. Each is measured at the 75th percentile of page loads, segmented across mobile and desktop. If your checklist still lists First Input Delay, throw it out. INP replaced FID as a Core Web Vital on 12 March 2024, and FID is gone.

![The three published Core Web Vitals thresholds: Largest Contentful Paint within 2.5 seconds, Interaction to Next Paint at 200 milliseconds or less, Cumulative Layout Shift at 0.1 or less, each measured at the 75th percentile of real page loads. First Input Delay is gone.](https://pixelstreet.in/blog/diagrams/features-of-website-core-web-vitals.svg)

Two things follow from that which nobody selling you a website will volunteer. Those thresholds are measured on real visitors, so a fast score on studio wi-fi proves nothing about a customer on a mid-range Android on a bad connection. And Lighthouse, the tool most agencies screenshot for you, bands its performance score 0 to 49 as poor, 50 to 89 as needs improvement, and 90 to 100 as good. Chrome’s own documentation says reaching 100 is “extremely challenging to achieve and not expected”. If someone has promised you a perfect score, they have promised you something Google does not ask for.

### Ways to Improve Website Speed:

- **Optimize images and videos:** Compress and resize media files to reduce their load time.
- **Minimize scripts and plugins:** Too many scripts and plugins can slow down your site. Keep only the essentials.
- **Use a reliable hosting service:** A good hosting provider can significantly impact your site’s speed.
- **Implement caching and CDNs:** Browser caching and content delivery networks help load your site faster for returning visitors.

## Search Engine Optimization (SEO) Basics

### How does SEO impact website visibility?

Optimising content, structure and technical elements is what makes a site findable. The full version is in [our SEO checklist](https://pixelstreet.in/blog/seo-checklist/); these are the parts that belong in a build.

### Basic SEO Tips:

- **Relevant keywords:** Use keywords that your target audience is searching for in your content.
- **Optimize meta titles and descriptions:** These appear in search engine results and can influence click-through rates.
- **Mobile-friendly design:** Not for a ranking bonus. For the reason given above — the mobile rendering is the one Google reads.
- **Improve site speed:** Faster websites tend to rank better on search engines.

## Calls to Action (CTAs)

### What makes a strong CTA?

A CTA tells a visitor what to do next: buy, book, subscribe, call. This is one of the three that decide the outcome, and the common failure is not a weak button. It is four competing CTAs on one screen, so none of them reads as the obvious next step.

### Tips for Crafting Effective CTAs:

- **Say the verb:** “Book a call” beats “Learn more”, which commits the reader to nothing.
- **Make one of them the loud one:** Contrast is how a reader knows which is primary.
- **Put it where the decision happens:** At the end of the section that made the argument, not only at the top.
- **Change one thing at a time** if you test, or you will not know what moved.

## High-Quality Content and Readability

### Why is content quality crucial for a website?

Content is the backbone of your website, and readability is part of quality rather than a separate job: clear hierarchy, short paragraphs, headings that say what the section contains.

### Tips for Effective Content:

- **Headlines that say something,** rather than headlines that tease it.
- **Short paragraphs.** A wall of text is a decision to skip.
- **Images that carry information,** not stock photographs of people pointing at laptops.
- **Update the pages that matter,** not everything on a schedule.

## Contact Information and Social Media Links

### Why should contact information and social media links be prominent?

A visitor who wants to contact you and cannot is the most expensive failure on this list, because they had already decided. Make the route obvious and make sure somebody answers it.

### Tips for Displaying Contact Information:

- **Same place on every page:** Header or footer, so nobody has to hunt for it.
- **Social icons in the footer,** not the header. They are exits, not entrances.
- **A form and an address,** not one or the other. Some people will not use a form, and forms break silently.

## Secure Socket Layer (SSL) Certificate

### What is an SSL certificate and why is it important?

An [**SSL certificate**](https://pixelstreet.in/blog/comprehensive-guide-to-ssl/) encrypts the data exchanged between a user’s browser and your website, providing a secure connection. This is especially important for websites that handle sensitive information, such as personal data or payment details, and it costs nothing for a standard site.

### Benefits of SSL Certificates:

- **Enhanced security:** Encrypts what your users send you. That is all it does, and it is not a statement about the business behind the site.
- **A ranking signal since 2014, and now just table stakes:** Google announced HTTPS as a ranking signal on its Online Security Blog in August 2014. Twelve years on, every competitor you have already did this. It wins you nothing. Not having it costs you.
- **Essential for e-commerce:** If you’re selling products or services online, an SSL certificate is a must.

## Live Chat Support

### How does live chat improve user experience?

Live chat provides immediate assistance to visitors, which can enhance their experience and increase conversions. It’s particularly useful for e-commerce sites, where users may have questions about products or shipping.

### Benefits of Live Chat:

- **Instant support:** Answer the question while the person is still on the page.
- **Increases conversions:** Guide users through the purchasing process with live assistance.
- **Enhances customer satisfaction:** Provide a direct line of communication, making users feel valued.

## User Testimonials and Reviews

### Do testimonials impact user trust?

Yes, and specificity is what makes them work. A named client with a result attached is evidence. An unattributed line of praise is decoration.

### How to Effectively Use Testimonials:

- **Strategic placement:** Place testimonials on key pages, such as the homepage or product pages, where they’ll have the most impact.
- **Use authentic photos:** Include customer photos and names to make the testimonials more credible.
- **Highlight positive reviews:** Showcase glowing reviews to reinforce the value of your offerings.

## Analytics and Tracking Tools

### Why are analytics important for a website?

Analytics tools like Google Analytics provide valuable insights into user behavior, helping you understand what’s working and what needs improvement. This data can guide your content strategy, marketing efforts, and overall website optimization. One caution from having sat through a lot of monthly reports: the metric people argue about hardest is usually the one they have the wrong definition of.

### Key Metrics to Track:

- **Page views and unique visitors:** Understand how many people are visiting your site.
- **Bounce rate, as GA4 actually defines it:** the share of sessions that were _not_ engaged, where an engaged session is one lasting longer than ten seconds, or firing a key event, or reaching two or more pages. It is no longer the old single-page-visit number, so a bounce rate that looks alarming next to a report from five years ago may be measuring something else entirely. It is also not a ranking signal, whatever the audit says.
- **Conversion rates:** Measure how effectively your site converts visitors into customers or leads.

## About Us Page

### What makes an About Us page effective?

Your About Us page is where you tell your story and name the people doing the work. Treat it as a sales page, because for anybody comparing three suppliers it already is one.

### Tips for Crafting a Compelling About Us Page:

- **Tell Your Story:** Share your brand’s history, mission, and values. Be authentic and let your personality shine through.
- **Introduce Your Team:** Include bios and photos of your team members. This humanizes your brand and helps visitors connect with the people behind the business.
- **Use Visuals:** Incorporate images or videos that tell your brand’s story, such as photos from company events or milestones.

## Footer with Essential Links

### What should be included in a website footer?

The footer holds what is not worth a menu slot: policies, contact details, a route to the rest of the site. Get it right and nobody notices, which is the correct outcome.

### Essential Footer Elements:

- **Links to key pages:** Include links to your privacy policy, terms of service, and other important pages.
- **Contact information:** Reiterate your contact details and social media links.
- **Sitemap:** Provide a link to a sitemap or include links to major sections of your site.

## Blog

### Why should your website have a blog?

A blog earns traffic only if it says something a competitor could not have written. That is the whole test, and most business blogs fail it.

### Tips for a Successful Blog:

- **Focus on Quality:** Create informative, engaging, and relevant content to your target audience. Avoid fluff and aim for value.
- **SEO Optimization:** Use SEO best practices to ensure your blog posts rank well on search engines. This includes using relevant keywords, optimizing meta descriptions, and including internal and external links.
- **Publish when you have something:** A quiet blog beats a padded one, and a padded one is now a liability rather than a neutral.

## FAQs

### How can an FAQ section benefit your website?

[![FAQ section feature in website](https://pixelstreet.in/blog/media/2024/09/Screenshot_1.png)](https://pixelstreet.in/blog/media/2024/09/Screenshot_1.png)

A well-organised FAQ section answers common questions, which reduces the number of people who have to email you to get started. One correction worth making, because a lot of advice on this has not caught up. FAQ sections used to earn an expanded rich result in Google. That ended: Google deprecated FAQ rich results on 7 May 2026 and they no longer appear in search. FAQPage markup is still valid schema and does no harm, so leave it on. But write the FAQ because a reader has the question, not because it used to win you extra pixels on a results page.

### Tips for a Helpful FAQ Section:

- **Identify Common Questions:** Think about the most frequent inquiries you receive and address them in your FAQ section.
- **Keep It Concise:** Provide clear and concise answers. Visitors are looking for quick solutions, so avoid long-winded explanations.
- **Organize by Category:** If your FAQ section is extensive, organize it into categories to make it easier for users to find what they’re looking for.

## Search Functionality

### Why is search functionality important?

A search bar helps visitors find things without navigating for them. It is repeated everywhere that studies show users who search are more likely to convert. I looked for a study I could name and did not find one, so that is not a claim I will make. What I will say as a recommendation rather than a finding: read your site search log. It is a list of things people expected to find on your site and did not, which makes it the cheapest customer research available to you.

### Tips for Implementing Search Functionality:

- **Make It Accessible:** Place the search bar in a prominent location, such as at the top right corner of the page, where users expect to find it.
- **Optimize for Speed:** Ensure that your search functionality is fast and efficient. Slow or inaccurate search results can frustrate users.
- **Include Filters:** If you have a large amount of content, consider adding filters or categories to help users refine their searches.

## Final Thoughts

Work through the fifteen hygiene items and you will have a website that does not embarrass you. That is a real achievement and most sites do not clear it. But it is not the same as a website that earns anything, and I would rather you knew the difference before you spent money. If the list above reads as a catalogue of things your current site fails, the question has stopped being which features to add and become whether to rebuild, which is a different decision with [its own warning signs](https://pixelstreet.in/blog/urgent-signs-for-website-redesign/).

The three that decide the outcome are the ones a checklist cannot help you with. What you say that no competitor could say. Whether it holds up on a mid-range phone on a bad connection. Whether a visitor knows what to do next. None of those is a feature you switch on; each is a decision somebody has to make about your specific business, which is also the argument in [template versus custom web design](https://pixelstreet.in/blog/template-vs-custom-web-design/) and the reason it matters who makes it.
